Round 2 — the panel earns its keep

The cold physics auditor convicted the science layer's temperature normalization: 6.3×10⁷ K was ~7× super-Eddington for the Schwarzschild thin disk this renderer actually integrates. The honest derivation (Ṁ = LEdd/ηc², η = 1−√(8/9), rin = 6GM/c²) gives 8.6×10⁶ K at 10 M☉ — now rendered and displayed. An image reviewer caught the plunging-region streams as dead code (the disk envelope's early-return executed first); they now render as visible infall between shadow and ISCO. Also fixed: Keplerian advection at the exact ΩK = √(M/r³), and interpolated photon direction at disk crossings (the step-start direction was stale mid-step — wrong Doppler sign possible near periapsis).
